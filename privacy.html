Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

VittaBridge ("we", "us", "our") operates vittabridge.com and provides real estate advisory services to NRI clients. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it deliberately plain: we collect only what we need to respond to you and advise you well, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you give us. When you submit an enquiry through our contact forms, email us, or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you provide. This typically includes your name, email address, phone number, country of residence, and the content of your message, such as the type of property or service you are interested in.
  • Information collected automatically. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, the approximate location of visitors, and the device and browser used. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the advisory services you request
  • To communicate with you about your engagement, including by email, phone or WhatsApp
  • To understand how our website is used so we can improve it
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations that apply to our work

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not send marketing communications unless you have asked to receive them.

Cookies and Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to distinguish visitors and measure site usage. The information generated is transmitted to and stored by Google. You can prevent this by disabling cookies in your browser or installing Google's opt-out browser add-on. The site works normally with cookies disabled.

Who We Share Information With

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only in these circumstances:

  • Professional partners. Where your engagement requires it and with your knowledge, we share relevant details with the chartered accountants, lawyers or other professionals who assist with your transaction.
  • Service providers. We use third-party services to operate the website and communicate with you, such as our hosting provider and Google Analytics. These providers process data on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information where required by law, regulation or a valid legal process.

Third-Party Links

Our website links to external services such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Google Maps, and our auction listings reference third-party auction portals. Once you leave our site, the privacy practices of those services apply, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Data Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and, where an engagement follows, for the duration of that engagement plus any period required by law. If you would like your details removed sooner, contact us and we will delete them unless we are legally required to retain them.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we limit access to your information to those who need it to serve you.

Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to:

  • Confirm what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Delete your information, subject to any legal retention requirements
  • Stop contacting you

To exercise any of these rights, email us at Sri@vittabridge.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed at adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected here, and continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about how we handle your information can be sent to Sri@vittabridge.com or raised with us on +91 97403 11355. VittaBridge is based in Bengaluru, India.
